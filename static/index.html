One tap, with the reason

This server keeps a run for one hour after its last activity (it starting, or your last approve or decline), and for 24 hours when a payment in it is waiting for you. Download the carbon copies to keep them.

When a money move needs the owner, Carbon Copy holds it and sends this card. Approve, and it goes to the PayPal stand-in right then. Decline, and it never does.

Every reason on a card is built only from facts Carbon Copy looked up: the list of suppliers she pays, her phone log, and the PayPal stand-in's records of the order, the payment, the refunds and the delivery tracking.
